# Locked Visual Baseline

Direction E, Habitat Journey, is locked after Gate A.

- Palette: warm white, lagoon teal, coral, sunny yellow, leaf green, deep navy.
- Typography: Nunito Sans UI and metrics; Fraunces for habitat and celebration display only.
- Surfaces: white rounded cards on a warm-white canvas; deep-navy navigation surface allowed.
- Density: comfortable 8-point rhythm with 16 px default page gutters.
- Shape: 12 px controls, 20 px feature cards, pills only for filters and status.
- Iconography: 24 px rounded 2 px stroke; selected navigation may be filled.
- Data visualization: direct labels and units; state never communicated by color alone.
- Illustration: consistent 2.5D animal and food art; maximum one-third of functional screens.

Reference boards: the selected direction board plus the core nutrition and habitat/progress screen boards.
