# Evaluated Design Directions

1. **Woodland Wellness** — warm forest palette and red-panda guide; broad wellness positioning.
2. **Active Animal Crew** — high-energy sport palette and multi-character coaching team.
3. **Cozy Kitchen Stories** — hand-drawn capybara chef and recipe-journal framing.
4. **Moonlight Minimal** — restrained premium interface with a subtle black-cat companion.
5. **Habitat Journey** — clean calorie tracking paired with habitat restoration and unlockable animal friends.

## Selected direction

**Habitat Journey** is selected. It offers the clearest product differentiation while retaining a practical tracking hierarchy. The selection establishes the direction; it does not constitute Gate B approval or production verification.
