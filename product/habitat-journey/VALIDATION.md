# Canonical Design Validation

The five canonical screens preserve the locked palette, typography, spacing, shape, iconography, and data-visualization signatures. Their hierarchy is consistent with the interactive prototype and keeps nutrition information primary.

Local deterministic checks confirm substantive artifacts, parseable structured data, explicit labels, accessible descriptions, absence of prohibited placeholders, and required design-contract markers. This is pre-production design validation; it is not browser, native runtime, device, performance, or production-environment verification.
