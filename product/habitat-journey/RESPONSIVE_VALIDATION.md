# Responsive Validation Contract

- Primary target: portrait phone from 320 to 480 CSS px.
- Wider viewports center the phone prototype without changing product information architecture.
- At 320 px, cards reflow to one column and labels remain visible.
- Dynamic type and text zoom to 200% preserve content order and primary actions.
- No horizontal scrolling is required for Today, Food Details, Habitat, or Progress.
- Touch targets remain at least 44 × 44 px.
- Illustration yields space before nutrition data, navigation, labels, or error recovery controls.
