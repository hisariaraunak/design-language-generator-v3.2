# Habitat Journey Design Decisions

## Confirmed

- Direction: Habitat Journey, chosen by the product owner after reviewing five materially different concepts.
- Audience: adults who want an encouraging daily calorie tracker with optional gentle gamification.
- Density: comfortable, with nutrition metrics visible before decorative content.
- Primary character: Ollie, an otter explorer and non-judgmental guide.
- Initial platforms: iOS and Android portrait phones.
- Initial theme and locale: light and en-US, with localization-ready layouts.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA and native equivalents; 44 px minimum targets; reduced motion.
- Progression: real logging actions grant cosmetic Habitat XP; rewards never change nutrition logic.
- Content: encouraging and concrete; no moral labels for foods and no streak-loss punishment.

## Prototype hypothesis

Users will understand nutrition status without opening the Habitat tab, while the habitat and friend unlocks provide a reason to return and log consistently.

## Evaluation questions

1. Can users log a meal without interpreting XP or habitat mechanics?
2. Is the daily calorie state understood within five seconds?
3. Does the friend-unlock moment feel rewarding without delaying the next task?
4. Can users disable the game layer and retain a complete tracker?
