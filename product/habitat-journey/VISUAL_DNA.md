# Habitat Journey Visual DNA

## Source observations

Five concept boards explored a shared animal-character calorie tracker across woodland wellness, active fitness, cozy kitchen, premium nocturnal, and habitat-progression expressions. Habitat Journey was selected for its clean nutrition hierarchy, lagoon palette, otter companion, and restorative-world progression.

## Stable signatures

- **Palette:** warm white canvas, lagoon teal actions, coral calorie accents, yellow XP, leaf-green completion, deep-navy text.
- **Typography:** rounded humanist sans-serif for UI and metrics; expressive serif restricted to habitat and celebration headings.
- **Layout:** portrait phone, 8-point rhythm, white cards, generous vertical flow, fixed bottom navigation.
- **Shape:** rounded 12–20 px surfaces, circular progress, pill filters, circular friend avatars.
- **Illustration:** premium 2.5D cartoon animals and isolated food icons, consistent three-quarter lighting.
- **Hierarchy:** tracking data dominates; habitat illustration is constrained on functional screens.
- **Iconography:** rounded 2 px stroke at 24 px; selected navigation may use a filled icon.
- **Data visualization:** direct labels, visible units, restrained teal/coral/yellow mapping, never color-only.

## Drift constraints

- Do not introduce raw colors outside approved semantic tokens.
- Do not use mascot art behind, over, or instead of health information.
- Do not mix photoreal food or animals into the illustrated asset system.
- Do not turn tracking screens into a dense game HUD.
- Preserve spacing, shape, typography, accessible contrast, and data-label rules across screens.
