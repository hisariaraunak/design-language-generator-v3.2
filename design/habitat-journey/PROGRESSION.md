# Habitat Progression Model

## Core loop

1. The user records or completes a real nutrition action.
2. The tracker confirms the health data first.
3. Eligible actions award a small, clearly stated amount of Habitat XP.
4. XP advances habitat stages, quests, and friend introductions.
5. Rewards unlock cosmetic scenes and stories, never nutrition advantages.

## Initial XP schedule

| Action | XP | Limit |
|---|---:|---|
| Log a complete meal | 10 | Four per day |
| Meet hydration target | 15 | Once per day |
| Review daily summary | 5 | Once per day |
| Complete a weekly reflection | 30 | Once per week |
| Maintain logging consistency | 5 | Daily; no escalating streak pressure |

## Guardrails

- Do not award XP for eating fewer calories, losing weight rapidly, or skipping meals.
- Missing a day never removes XP, friends, habitat upgrades, or streak history.
- A streak records consistency but does not punish a break; the next day starts a new active run.
- Calorie goals and health recommendations must come from validated product logic, not the reward system.
- Users can mute celebrations and disable the habitat layer without losing tracker functionality.
