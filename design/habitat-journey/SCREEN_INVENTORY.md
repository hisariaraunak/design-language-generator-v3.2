# Core Screen Inventory

## Primary navigation

1. **Today** — calorie balance, macros, meals, daily quest, quick log entry.
2. **Log** — meal selection, search, barcode entry, recent foods, custom food.
3. **Progress** — calorie consistency, macro averages, weight trend, XP history.
4. **Habitat** — habitat state, friends, quests, upgrades, story moments.
5. **Profile** — goals, units, reminders, accessibility, integrations, account.

## Required supporting screens

- Onboarding: goals, dietary preferences, units, activity, companion introduction.
- Food details: serving size, portion unit, macros, meal destination.
- Meal review: selected items, total nutrition, save confirmation.
- Barcode result and no-result states.
- Custom food and saved meal creation.
- Weight logging and editing with date and kg/lb support.
- Weight history with 7-day, 1-month, and 3-month trends plus reversible deletion.
- Friend unlocked celebration.
- Friend collection and friend profile.
- Habitat upgrade preview and confirmation.
- Notification and reminder settings.
- Empty, loading, offline, permission-denied, and recoverable error states.
